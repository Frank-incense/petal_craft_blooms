Our Story

About Petal Craft Blooms

Born in Nairobi, Petal Craft Blooms started with a simple belief — every flower tells a story. We work with local growers and importers to bring you the freshest, most vibrant stems daily. Each arrangement is handcrafted by our team of passionate florists who understand that the smallest details make the biggest difference.

From intimate celebrations to grand gestures, we believe in delivering joy with every petal. Premium quality. Thoughtful design. Delivered with care.
